## 📝 Form Field Guide

### Field 1: Applicant ID 👤
**Description:** Your unique identifier in our system
**Format:** Text (auto-generated if empty)
**Examples:** APP001, APP2024001, LOAN001
**What to enter:** Leave blank or enter custom ID
**Info Icon:** Shows "Applicant ID is your unique identifier in our system. Auto-generated if left blank."

---

### Field 2: Age 🎂
**Description:** Your current age in years
**Format:** Slider (18-70)
**Valid Range:** 18 to 70 years
**Examples:** 25, 35, 45, 60
**Info Help:** "Your current age (18-70 years). Example: 30, 45, 65"
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "I'm 30 years old"

---

### Field 3: Location 📍
**Description:** Your city of residence
**Format:** Text input
**Supported Cities:** Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad (and any other city)
**Examples:** Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore
**Info Help:** "Your city of residence. Example: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore"
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "I'm in Mumbai"

---

### Field 4: Monthly Income 💵
**Description:** Your total monthly income after taxes
**Format:** Number (₹)
**Valid Range:** ₹1,000 - ₹10,000,000
**Examples:** ₹50,000, ₹100,000, ₹150,000, ₹500,000
**Info Help:** "Your total monthly income after taxes. Include salary, business income, etc."
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "I'm earning ₹120k monthly"

---

### Field 5: Employment Type 💼
**Description:** Your current employment status
**Format:** Dropdown (3 options)
**Options:**
- 🏢 Salaried (Regular job, permanent position)
- 🏪 Self-Employed (Business owner, freelancer)
- ❌ Unemployed (No current employment)
**Examples:** Salaried, Self-Employed
**Info Help:** "Employment status. Salaried: Regular job, Self-Employed: Business/Freelance"
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "I'm salaried" or "I have my own business"

---

### Field 6: Monthly Liabilities 📊
**Description:** Your monthly debt payments
**Format:** Number (₹)
**Valid Range:** ₹0 - ₹1,000,000
**Includes:** Car loan, other loans, credit card payments
**Examples:** ₹10,000, ₹25,000, ₹50,000
**Info Help:** "Your monthly debt payments (car loan, other loans, credit card). Example: ₹10,000"
**Default:** ₹15,000 (if not provided)
**Note:** Set to 0 if you have no existing debts

---

### Field 7: Credit Score 📈
**Description:** Your CIBIL or Credit Score
**Format:** Slider (300-900)
**Valid Range:** 300 to 900
**Score Levels:**
- 🔴 300-580: Poor/Very Poor
- 🟡 580-650: Fair
- 🟢 650-750: Good
- 🟢 750-900: Excellent
**Examples:** 580, 650, 720, 750, 800
**Info Help:** "Your CIBIL/credit score (300-900). Higher is better. Example: 580 (Poor), 700 (Good), 750+ (Excellent)"
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "my credit score is 720"

---

### Field 8: Loan Amount 💰
**Description:** Amount you want to borrow
**Format:** Number (₹)
**Valid Range:** ₹10,000 - ₹100,000,000
**Examples:** ₹200,000, ₹500,000, ₹1,000,000, ₹5,000,000
**Info Help:** "Amount you want to borrow. Example: ₹200,000, ₹500,000"
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "I need ₹500,000"

---

### Field 9: Tenure ⏳
**Description:** Loan repayment period
**Format:** Slider (6-360 months)
**Valid Range:** 6 to 360 months
**Convert:** 
- 6 months = 0.5 years
- 12 months = 1 year
- 60 months = 5 years
- 120 months = 10 years
- 360 months = 30 years
**Examples:** 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 84, 120, 180
**Info Help:** "Loan repayment period in months. Example: 12 (1 yr), 60 (5 yrs), 120 (10 yrs)"
**Auto-filled from:** Chat message like "I want a 5-year tenure"

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue 1: Chat scrolls but doesn't show latest message
**Solution:** Message appears at the bottom when scrolled. Browser auto-scrolls now.

### Issue 2: Form has mixed data types error
**Solution:** Now fixed with type conversion. All numbers properly converted to int.

### Issue 3: Error "This app has encountered an error"
**Solution:** 
- Refresh page (Ctrl+R)
- Clear chat and start fresh
- Check if API is running (http://localhost:8000/health)

### Issue 4: Form fields showing but empty
**Solution:** 
- Start chatting to auto-populate
- Or manually fill each field
- Click help icon (❓) for examples

### Issue 5: Can't extract information from chat
**Solution:** Use this format:
```
"I'm [age], earning ₹[income], [employment_type], credit score [score], need [amount] loan for [tenure] months"
```

## 🎯 Tips for Best Results

1. **Chat First**: Let the bot extract your info, then verify in the form

5. **Ask Questions**: The chatbot has FAQ responses for common questions
6. **Review Decisions**: Read the full decision with explanations
